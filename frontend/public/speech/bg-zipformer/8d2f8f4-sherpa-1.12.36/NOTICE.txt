Bulgarian local reservation speech recognition

Model: Bulgarian ASR Zipformer, Preslav Penev, Apache-2.0 (model card).
https://huggingface.co/preslaff/bulgarian-asr-zipformer
Revision: 8d2f8f4f159918a27b33c430e4fa83b10c705c28
Files are the unmodified dynamic int8 ONNX exports and token table.

Runtime: sherpa-onnx v1.12.36, k2-fsa / Xiaomi Corporation, Apache-2.0.
https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/releases/tag/v1.12.36
Official browser WASM archive: sherpa-onnx-wasm-simd-1.12.36-vad-asr-en-zipformer_gigaspeech.tar.bz2
The .wasm is unmodified. The JavaScript removes only the bundled English
.data preloader and exposes its RAM filesystem; the ASR wrapper exposes
OfflineRecognizer on the worker global. No English model is distributed.
Runtime includes ONNX Runtime (MIT) and k2-fsa dependencies; see upstream
https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/tree/v1.12.36/cmake for dependency licenses.

Recognition runs inside a dedicated Web Worker. Model/runtime files may be
cached; recordings and recognized text are never stored or uploaded.
Author benchmarks are not tablet validation. Validate on actual iPad hardware.
Reproduce/verify with scripts/prepare-reservation-local-speech.py.
